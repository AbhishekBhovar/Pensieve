Pensieve 2.0

Major interface rebuild:
- Inactive screens use display:none and cannot visually overlap.
- Exactly one screen is active at any time.
- Removed the free-write Home entry permanently.
- Home is choice-first only with the six feeling options.
- Bottom navigation hides during conversations and reappears only on Home/Reflections/Insights/More.
- Routing uses one tiny explicit controller and no layered :target UI.
- Loading remains 4 seconds with 0% and 100% endpoints and a filling bar.
- Locked Pensieve artwork and logo retained unchanged.
