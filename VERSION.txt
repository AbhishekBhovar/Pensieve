Pensieve 1.3

Critical interaction fix:
- Reworked iPhone/Safari tap handling.
- Buttons no longer rely only on inline onclick events.
- touchend is handled directly and mapped to app actions.
- Click remains as desktop/browser fallback.
- Loading overlay is physically removed from the DOM after 5 seconds.
- Added independent loading watchdog.
- Active controls are explicitly pointer-enabled.
- Locked Pensieve artwork remains unchanged.
